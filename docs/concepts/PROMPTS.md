# Concept art prompts

Prompts used for the "Forged in Damascus" battle scenes, kept so they can be
re-run on another generator (Hugging Face Qwen-Image, Figma `generate_image`).
Generated with Figma `gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image`, 1920×1080 requested.

## Yarmouk, 636 — `battle-yarmouk-636.jpg`

> Cinematic 3D game concept art, Battle of Yarmouk, 636 AD, in the Yarmouk river
> valley in southern Syria. Time is frozen mid-moment: Arab Rashidun cavalry in
> mail hauberks, turbans wrapped over iron helmets, with long lances and round
> shields, charging down a dusty slope on galloping horses with hooves in
> mid-air, toward a line of Eastern Roman soldiers in lamellar armour with plumed
> helmets and large round shields. A sandstorm blows across the field, dust and
> sand particles hang motionless in the air, dozens of arrows are suspended
> mid-flight. Deep basalt ravines and the river gorge in the background, harsh
> golden August sunlight, epic wide angle, volumetric light, stylized realism,
> Unreal Engine render. No blood, no gore, no text.

Known issue: the Roman side came out as 1st-century legionaries. Next run, add
"7th-century Byzantine, ridge helmets, mail and lamellar, no lorica segmentata,
no rectangular shields".

## Siege of Damascus, 1148 — `battle-siege-damascus-1148.jpg`

> Cinematic 3D game concept art, Siege of Damascus, 1148 AD, Second Crusade.
> Time is frozen mid-moment inside the dense orchards of the Ghouta just west of
> the city: Damascene Turkish horse archers in lamellar armour and fur-trimmed
> caps ride between apricot and walnut trees and low mud-brick garden walls, bows

> towers of Damascus with the tall minarets of the Great Umayyad Mosque, the
> Barada river glinting. Warm late-afternoon light through the trees, dust
> shafts, epic wide angle, stylized realism, Unreal Engine render. No blood, no
> gore, no text.

## Shadowbox diorama — `shadowbox-diorama.jpg`

> Concept art for an interactive website: a deep illuminated paper-cut shadowbox
> diorama seen through a glass screen, viewed slightly from the side so the depth
> is obvious. Eight separated layers of hand-cut black and deep-indigo paper
> silhouettes recede into the box: tall pine trees in front, rolling hills, a
> winding path, a tiny lighthouse and village, distant mountains, and a large
> paper moon at the back. Warm golden backlight glows between the layers,
> rim-lighting every cut edge, soft haze and floating dust motes. Visible paper
> texture and slightly imperfect hand-cut edges. Minimal, magical, quiet night
> mood. High detail, cinematic lighting, no text.

Known issue: the model wrapped the image in a fake video-player UI; it was
cropped out. Add "no user interface, no browser chrome" next time.
